Color Snatch

Type: Solo Project

Genre: Action, Competitive Multiplayer, Arcade, Real-Time PvP, Strategy

The game is a competitive two-player experience designed around real-time interaction and strategic decision-making. Players compete within a shared play area to collect and steal colored balls while simultaneously avoiding roaming enemy entities. The gameplay emphasizes fast-paced mechanics, requiring players to demonstrate quick reaction times, spatial awareness, and tactical planning. The presence of autonomous enemies introduces additional challenge and unpredictability, encouraging adaptive strategies and risk management. Overall, the game promotes competitive engagement through dynamic player interaction and time-sensitive objectives.
