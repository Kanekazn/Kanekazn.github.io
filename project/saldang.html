SALDANG: The Lost Soul of Bicol

Genre: 2D Roguelike

Award: Mythic Bicol Game Jam 2025

Enter the ever-changing world of Ibalon in this roguelike inspired by Bicolano mythology. Fight mythical enemies, explore procedural maps, and restore the Lost Soul of Bicol.

Key Features

  • Fast-paced combat
